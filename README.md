# indiana-plans
This project uses the 2026 QHP Landscape file for the Individual Medical market from data.healthcare.gov to look at Indiana health plans. After cleaning the data and keeping one entry per plan and rating area, 883 plans remained. The scatter plot in this repository, premium_vs_deductible.png, shows the monthly premium for a 40 year old adult on the horizontal axis and the individual medical deductible on the vertical axis. The graph slopes downward, which means plans with higher deductibles tend to have lower monthly premiums. Plans with a deductible of zero cost much more each month, with premiums from roughly 550 to over 1,300 dollars, while plans with deductibles near 10,000 dollars cost roughly 370 to 550 dollars. This is the risk tradeoff from class, since buyers who pick a high deductible pay less each month but carry more of the financial risk themselves, and more risk averse buyers would be expected to choose the low deductible and high premium end. The graph was made with the help of an AI tool, and the code is in make_graph.py.
